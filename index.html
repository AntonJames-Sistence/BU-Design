Who we are

BU Design is a New York-based consultancy specializing in architecture, communications, business development, and strategic positioning. Our mission is to elevate you—architects, landscape architects, designers, builders, and specialty subcontractors—ensuring that your unique vision and voice are seen, heard, and celebrated within the industry. Whether you're shaping spaces, creating innovative products, or providing specialized construction services, we're committed to fostering your growth and success, amplifying the impact of your diverse perspectives in every project you touch.
